Hi, I'm Jelmer Snoeck, a Canadian software engineer living in Squamish, BC.

I write about distributed systems, Kubernetes, and (more recently) agent identity and the security shape of AI infrastructure. I work at [Keycard](https://keycard.ai), where we're building a control plane for autonomous AI agents: identity, policy, scoped credentials, audit. Before that I spent a lot of time in Kubernetes and platform engineering.

If you're new here and want to start somewhere:

- [From Desired State to Negotiated State](/posts/from-desired-state-to-negotiated-state/). Why the control loop that built Kubernetes doesn't fit agents, and what replaces it.
- [Agents Are Not One Thing](/posts/agents-are-not-one-thing/). The word "agent" covers at least four different systems with very different risk shapes. A vocabulary for telling them apart.
- [Deploying Rock Solid Applications with Kubernetes](/posts/deploying-rock-solid-applications-with-kubernetes/). The operational checklist I wish I'd had earlier.
